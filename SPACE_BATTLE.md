# Level 28 - SPACE BATTLE (v43)
- Opens with a handwritten note: "I can't defeat him on land... I will destroy him in SPACE."
- 3D space dogfight: your hover has twin mini guns (hold SPACE / click / FIRE button), the UFO fires telegraphed lasers (red warning lines, then bolts, fan volleys and a sweeping beam in later phases).
- 3 hits = shot down and the level resets. UFO has a health bar floating above it (160 hits, 3 phases: faster/deadlier at 66% and 33%).
- Controls: A/D or arrows = left/right, W/S = up/down, SPACE or mouse click = fire. Touch: joystick + FIRE button.
- Code: game/js/game/dogfight.js, game/css/dogfight.css. Unlocks after Level 27.
